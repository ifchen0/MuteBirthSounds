[h2]Now part of Vanilla Tune-Up[/h2]This mod has been merged into [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815774199]Vanilla Tune-Up: Fixes & Tweaks[/url] as the [b]Mute labor and birth sounds[/b] tweak, and will not be updated further. You can switch at any time: while this mod is active, that part of Vanilla Tune-Up is skipped, so nothing is applied twice. Start the game once with both mods active and this tweak stays on after you remove this mod; otherwise turn it on in Vanilla Tune-Up's mod settings.
[hr][/hr]
The labor and birth sounds can be unsettling and irritating, so this mod mutes them.

Mutes the labor (early/late) and birth sounds played while a pawn is giving birth (Biotech). Other baby sounds are unchanged. XML patch only, safe to add or remove at any time.
